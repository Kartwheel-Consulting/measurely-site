Measurely — measurement-based pricing for Shopify

Sell by the metre and charge exactly that. Customers type their own size on the product page, the price follows the measurement as they type, and checkout charges exactly that price, recalculated on Shopify's side.

Product tour

A 40-second video: a merchant creates a calculator at $85 per square metre and applies it to glass products; a customer types a size and the price follows — $170.00 for 200 × 100 cm, the $25.00 product price for a small 20 × 10 cm cut, and $61.20 for 120 × 60 cm — through to the cart, where Shopify recalculates the price at checkout.

Nine ways to measure

  • Area — length × width: for glass, worktops, signage and blinds cut from a sheet.
  • Area — direct entry: for flooring, wallpaper and turf ordered by the square metre or square foot.
  • Length: for rope, cable, chain and fabric by the metre.
  • Linear — length + width: for L-shaped runs such as worktop edging.
  • Perimeter: for frames, borders and edging.
  • Volume — length × width × height: for foam, timber and packaging.
  • Volume — direct entry: for soil, aggregates and concrete.
  • Weight: priced per kilogram or per pound.
  • Quantity: a price per item with floors, caps and discounts.

Features

  • Metric and imperial units converted automatically — customers can type centimetres while you price per square foot.
  • The product's own price is always the floor, so small cuts are never under-charged.
  • Prices recalculated at checkout — the browser's figure is never trusted.
  • Wastage allowance and rounding, size limits and step sizes.
  • Tiered rates by size and quantity discounts.
  • A full history of every change that affects a price.
